Household Budget

Privacy policy and SMS terms · Last updated August 2026

This is a private application used by one household. It is not a product, it is not offered to the public, and it has no customers. It is used by two people: the owner and their spouse.

What the app is

Household Budget is a single-file personal budgeting tool. It tracks recurring bills, paychecks, and credit card spending for one family, and sends one text message each evening summarising where the month stands.

Who receives messages

Two people: the account owner and their spouse. No one else can be added, because there is no signup, no mailing list, and no way for a stranger to reach the application.

How consent is given

  • Each recipient types their own mobile number into the app's settings screen. Entering the number is what turns the messages on.
  • A number can only be entered by someone already using the app on their own device.
  • Consent was additionally given verbally between the two household members.

How to stop messages

  • Reply STOP to any message. Sending stops immediately.
  • Or remove the number from the app's settings screen.
  • Reply HELP for contact information.

Message and data rates may apply. Messages are sent once per day, in the evening.

What is collected

  • Phone numbers — the two household members' own numbers, stored only to send the nightly summary.
  • Budget figures — stored in a private GitHub gist belonging to the account owner, and on their own devices.

What is not done with it

  • Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with any third party.
  • No advertising, no marketing, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No data is collected from anyone outside the household, because no one outside the household uses the application.

Message delivery is handled by Twilio, and bank balances are read through SimpleFIN. Both are service providers acting on the account owner's behalf.

Retention

Budget data is kept as long as the household uses the app, and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Phone numbers are removed as soon as they are taken out of the app's settings.
