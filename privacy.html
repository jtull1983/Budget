Household Budget

Privacy Policy and SMS Terms and Conditions · Last updated August 2026

Household Budget is a personal budgeting application operated by an individual for the private use of a single household. It tracks recurring bills, income, and credit card spending, and sends one informational text message each evening summarizing the household's own budget position.

It is not offered to the public and has no customers. There are exactly two message recipients: the individual who operates the application and their spouse.

Privacy Policy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Information sharing to subcontractors in support services, such as customer service, is permitted. All other use case categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Information collected

  • Mobile phone numbers — the two household members' own numbers, collected solely to deliver the nightly budget summary.
  • Budget information — recurring bills, income, and credit card transaction data belonging to the household, stored in a private GitHub gist controlled by the operator and in local storage on the household's own devices.

How information is used

Mobile phone numbers are used for one purpose only: delivering the nightly SMS summary described in these terms. Budget information is used only to produce that summary and to display the household's budget within the application.

How information is shared

Mobile information and SMS consent are not sold, rented, or shared with any third party or affiliate for marketing or promotional purposes, and are not shared with any third party for any other purpose.

Two service providers process data strictly on the operator's behalf in order to make the application function:

  • Twilio — transmits the SMS messages. Twilio receives the recipient phone numbers solely to deliver those messages.
  • SimpleFIN — provides read-only access to the household's own credit card transactions.

Neither provider is permitted to use this information for marketing, and neither receives SMS opt-in data or consent records for any purpose other than delivering the messages described here.

Data retention and deletion

Budget information is retained for as long as the household uses the application, and may be deleted by the operator at any time. A mobile number is removed immediately when it is deleted from the application's settings screen or when the recipient replies STOP.

Security

Budget data is held in a private GitHub gist accessible only with the operator's personal access token. Credentials are never stored in the application file itself.

SMS Terms and Conditions

Program description

Household Budget sends one informational text message per day to the two members of the household that operates it. Each message reports the household's current credit card spending for the billing cycle, its position against the household's own budget, and the projected balance at the end of the month. These messages are informational only. They contain no marketing, no promotional content, no offers, and no third-party content of any kind.

How recipients opt in

There is no public sign-up, because the application is not offered to the public. Every opt-in path is listed here in full:

  • In-application entry. A recipient opens the Household Budget application on their own device, navigates to the Sync settings screen, and types their own mobile number into the notification field. The field is empty by default and nothing is pre-selected or pre-checked. Entering a number and saving it is the affirmative act of consent. Adjacent to that field, the application displays: "Household Budget will send you one budget summary text per day. Up to 1 msg/day. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. See our Privacy Policy and Terms."
  • Verbal. The operator's spouse additionally gave verbal consent, in person, to receive the nightly summary before her number was entered.

No number can be entered by anyone who is not already using the application on their own device. There is no web form, no keyword opt-in, no QR code, no paper form, and no purchased or third-party list.

Message frequency

Up to one message per day per recipient.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply.

How to stop messages

Reply STOP to any message at any time to cancel. Recipients will receive a confirmation message and no further messages will be sent. Recipients may also remove their number from the application's settings screen. To resume, reply START.

How to get help

Reply HELP to any message, or email the address in the Contact section below.

Eligibility and acceptable use

  • Figures are estimates drawn from the household's own records and read-only bank data. The application does not provide financial advice and carries no warranty.
